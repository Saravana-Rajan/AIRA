AIRA for Android

Version 1.0.0 · 2.2 MB · Android 7.0 or newer

The app always loads the latest AIRA. No reinstall needed for updates.

Download Android v1.0.0 Or open AIRA in the browser

Installation

  1. Tap Download Android v1.0.0 on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded AIRA.apk from the notification or the Files app.
  3. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser or Files app.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Tap Start once and allow the microphone and camera. After that, AIRA starts listening as soon as you open it.

Requirements

  • Android 7.0 or newer, with Google Chrome installed.
  • Internet connection (mobile data or Wi-Fi).
  • Microphone and camera access.

On iPhone, open aira-shop.web.app in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen.
