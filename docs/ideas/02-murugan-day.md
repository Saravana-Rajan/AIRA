# A day at Murugan Dairy with AIRA

**Murugan, 38, is blind. He runs *Murugan Dairy*, a small shop in Chennai selling Aavin milk, Sakthi curd and Arun ice cream.** He started it with an NHFDC self-employment loan. Until now his nephew helped with stock, bills and money. Today Murugan runs the shop alone.

**Setup:** his phone sits in a **chest mount** with the camera facing forward, and he wears one earphone. **Double-tap** anywhere on the screen to talk, **long-press** to stop. AIRA speaks Tamil and English, mixed the way he does.

---

### 6:30 AM: opening, and ordering stock
Murugan unlocks the shutter and double-taps.

> **Murugan:** "AIRA, Sakthi curd stock evvalavu?" (how much stock?)
> **AIRA:** "Sakthi curd: 4 litres recorded. You sell about 26 litres a day."
> **Murugan:** "30 litres Sakthi curd order pannu, Sakthi vendor-ku." (order it)
> **AIRA:** "Order 30 litres Sakthi curd from Ramesh, the Sakthi vendor, on WhatsApp. Shall I send it?"
> **Murugan:** "Yes."
> **AIRA:** "Sent." *(soft done chime)*

A few minutes later:

> **AIRA:** "Ramesh replied: 'OK, delivery by 8:30.'"

### 8:35 AM: delivery arrives, 2 short
The van arrives. Murugan puts the curd packs into fridge 2 one by one, holding each pack up to the camera for a moment.

> **AIRA:** "One… two… three…" *(a soft tick with each count)* "…twenty-seven, twenty-eight."
> **Murugan:** "That's all of it."
> **AIRA:** "28 litres counted. The order was 30, so **2 litres short**. Shall I record 28 received?"
> **Murugan:** "Yes, 28."
> **AIRA:** "Recorded 28 litres. Shortfall noted against Ramesh."

### 8:40 AM: the vendor's bill doesn't match
Ramesh hands over a paper bill. Murugan holds it in front of the camera.

> **AIRA:** "Hold it still… reading twice… The bill says **30 litres** Sakthi curd at ₹… a litre, total ₹…. But only 28 arrived. **The bill is ₹… too high.**"
> **Murugan:** *(to Ramesh)* "Ramesh, 28 dhaan vandhuchu. Bill-a correct pannunga." (Only 28 came. Please correct the bill.)

Ramesh corrects it by hand.

> **AIRA:** "New bill: 28 litres, total ₹…. Now it matches the delivery ✓."

### 8:45 AM: paying the vendor in cash
> **Murugan:** "Pay Ramesh in cash."
> **AIRA:** "Pay ₹… in cash to Ramesh? Confirm."
> **Murugan:** "Yes."
> **AIRA:** "₹500 notes are in the left slot of the cashbox. Take the notes and show them to me." … "I see two ₹500 notes, ₹1,000. You need ₹… more. ₹100 notes are in the middle slot." … "Correct, ₹…. Give it to Ramesh."

The payment is recorded as **confirmed**, and the delivery is **closed** with the shortfall on record.

### 8:50 AM: teaching AIRA where things are
> **Murugan:** *(hand on the shelf)* "AIRA, idhu Sakthi curd shelf: fridge 2, middle shelf, left."
> **AIRA:** "Saved. Sakthi curd: fridge 2, middle shelf, left."

### 10:15 AM: a customer, guided to the right pack
> **Customer:** "Anna, rendu litre curd kudunga." (Two litres of curd, please.)
> **AIRA:** *(in Murugan's ear)* "2 litres Sakthi curd. 26 in stock. Fridge 2, middle shelf, left."

Murugan opens fridge 2. A guidance tone starts: higher pitch means move up, and the sound moves left or right.

> **AIRA:** "A little left… down… stop. That's **Sakthi curd 1 litre** ✓. Take one more." … "Two packs ✓. Total ₹…."

### 10:16 AM: cash payment, change checked
The customer hands over a note. Murugan shows it to the camera.

> **AIRA:** "₹200 note. Change is ₹…. Show me the change before you give it." … "₹… ✓. Correct change."

The sale is completed. **Stock: 24 litres**, deducted exactly once.

### 11:40 AM: UPI payment confirmed by the soundbox
A college student buys an Arun ice cream box and pays by UPI. The shop's payment soundbox announces the payment aloud.

> **Soundbox:** "Received rupees one hundred and twenty."
> **AIRA:** "UPI ₹120 received ✓, matches the sale."

Another customer shows Murugan a payment screenshot, but the soundbox stays silent.

> **AIRA:** "No soundbox confirmation yet. **Payment not confirmed.** Ask them to pay again or wait."

### 4:30 PM: low stock and expiry
> **AIRA:** "Sakthi curd is down to **2 litres**. You usually sell 26 a day. Shall I order 30 litres for tomorrow morning?"
> **Murugan:** "Yes, order pannu."
> **AIRA:** "Order sent to Ramesh on WhatsApp."
> **AIRA:** "Also, **6 Aavin milk packets expire tomorrow**. Sell them first. They're on the top shelf of fridge 1."

### 9:00 PM: closing the shop
> **Murugan:** "AIRA, inniki eppadi?" (How was today?)
> **AIRA:** "**48 sales, ₹2,860.** Top seller: Aavin milk. Ramesh's delivery was 2 litres short and the bill was corrected. Tomorrow's suggested order: **30 L curd, 20 L milk**. Good night, Murugan."

His nephew checks the **Shop Dashboard** from home. He isn't needed at the shop any more.
