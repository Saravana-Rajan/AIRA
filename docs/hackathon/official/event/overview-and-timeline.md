# Overview and timeline

Checked: 2026-10-06. Source: [official overview](https://aibuildercup.com/).

Google Cloud AI Builder Cup is a JAPAC hackathon independently operated by Hack2skill and sponsored by Google Cloud. Teams build solutions for real problems, with shortlisted teams demonstrating them to experts in Singapore.

## Schedule

| Milestone | Published dates (2026) |
| --- | --- |
| Registration and team formation | September 1–October 11 |
| Prototype development and submission | September 7–October 18 |
| Submission deadline | October 18 |
| Evaluation | October 19–November 6 |
| Finalist announcement | November 7 |
| In-person finale, Singapore | December 4 |
