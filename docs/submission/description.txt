AIRA lets a blind shopkeeper run a small shop alone. He wears a chest-mounted phone and talks in Tamil, Hindi or English. Gemini Live (gemini-3.8-live) holds the conversation and calls tools; Gemini Robotics-ER 2 counts delivered packs, identifies products and guides his hand to the right item; Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.6 Flash read invoices and cash independently, and deterministic code checks every number. Cloud Run hosts aira-live (FastAPI + Google ADK), the voice orchestrator and business engine. The model proposes, the backend commits: stock and money change only after a spoken yes, in Firestore transactions with idempotency keys. Firestore is the system of record: products, orders, deliveries, stock ledger, sales, payments, spatial memory and audit trail; its sales ledger drives demand forecasts. Firebase Hosting serves the phone app and the Shop Dashboard, which streams live Firestore data via the Firebase SDK. Orders go to vendors on WhatsApp; UPI is confirmed by the shop's payment soundbox.
