AIRA lets a blind shopkeeper run a small shop alone. He wears a chest-mounted phone and talks in any language; AIRA answers in it and follows every switch, turn by turn (demo: Tamil and English). Gemini Live (gemini-3.8-live) holds the conversation and calls tools; Gemini Robotics-ER 2 counts delivered packs, identifies products and guides his hand to the item; Gemini 3.8 Flash and 3.6 Flash read invoices and cash independently; deterministic code checks every number. Cloud Run hosts aira-live (FastAPI + Google ADK): voice orchestrator and business engine. The model proposes, the backend commits: stock and money change only after a spoken yes, in Firestore transactions with idempotency keys. Firestore holds products, orders, deliveries, stock, sales, payments, spatial memory and the audit trail; its sales ledger drives demand forecasts. Firebase Hosting serves the phone app and the Shop Dashboard, streaming live Firestore data. Orders go to vendors on Telegram; UPI is confirmed by the payment soundbox.
