## Conflicts and missing details

- Challenges list six industry themes, but their submission-category section instead mentions healthcare, education, sustainability, accessibility, and social good. Confirm category selection with support.
- Challenges mention a three-minute demo; FAQs and terms require less than three minutes. The supplied dashboard additionally says 3–4 minutes, while its field says up to 3 minutes. These conflict; obtain organizer clarification before finalizing the video.
- Deployment wording varies between Cloud Run/Firebase and broader GCP references. Cloud Run/Firebase satisfies the narrower published wording.
- See [timeline](../event/overview-and-timeline.md) for deadline ambiguity and [prizes](../event/prizes.md) for the zero-value heading discrepancy.
- No exhaustive eligible-country list, detailed session timetable, named judging panel, or exact finale venue was established from these sources.

Recheck official pages before submission. Direct unresolved questions to the support contact in the overview.
