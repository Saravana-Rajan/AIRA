# Eligibility and practical FAQs

Checked: 2026-10-06. Source: [official FAQs](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html).

## Team rules

- Participants must be 21+, based in JAPAC, and working professionals, entrepreneurs, or startup participants.
- Students are excluded; one student can disqualify the whole team, including after selection.
- Teams require 2–4 members. Individual registration is allowed while finding teammates.
- Cross-country JAPAC teams are allowed; members outside JAPAC are excluded.
- Each person joins one team; each team submits one solution under one theme.
- Replace or add teammates only during registration, ending October 11.

## Building

- Participation is free throughout.
- Build a fresh project during the event; existing projects are ineligible.
- An end-to-end prototype is sufficient. Primarily using another cloud makes it ineligible.
- Submit a deployed URL, public GitHub repository, explanatory deck, and demo shorter than three minutes.
- Online program/problem-statement sessions provide support.

## Finale

Only two representatives may attend, even if others self-fund. Sponsorship covers flights, accommodation, and finale-day meals. Attendees handle visas and their costs; last-minute traveler substitutions cannot be accommodated.

A preliminary scoring round selects finalists. Judges will be announced later; all themes share one rubric.
