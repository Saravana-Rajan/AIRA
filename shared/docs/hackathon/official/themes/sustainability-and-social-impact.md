# Sustainability & Social Impact

Source: user-supplied challenge text, received 2026-10-06. Preserved as supplied content; technology suggestions have not been independently validated against current product documentation.

## Problem statement

Build an AI-powered solution that addresses a meaningful environmental or societal challenge. Solutions may focus on improving resource efficiency, increasing accessibility, strengthening resilience, supporting communities, or enabling more informed and sustainable decision-making.

## Suggested solution directions

Climate and environmental intelligence; disaster preparedness; sustainable agriculture; resource optimization; accessibility; community services; waste reduction.

These are suggested options, not a requirement to use every service. Roles below summarize the supplied descriptions.
