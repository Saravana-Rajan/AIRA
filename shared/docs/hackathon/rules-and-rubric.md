## Our theme: Sustainability & Social Impact
> "Build an AI-powered solution that addresses a meaningful environmental or societal challenge. Solutions may focus on improving resource efficiency, **increasing accessibility**, strengthening resilience, supporting communities, or enabling more informed and sustainable decision-making."

- **Suggested directions** include **accessibility** and community services.
- **Suggested Google Cloud tech** (all of these map into AIRA, except Earth Engine):
  - Gemini on Vertex AI (now Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform)
  - BigQuery
  - Vertex AI predictive models
  - Earth Engine
  - Cloud Storage
  - Vision AI
  - Cloud Run
  - Pub/Sub

## Judging rubric
| Weight | Criterion | What judges look for |
|---|---|---|
| **40%** | **Technical Merit & Gen AI Implementation** | Meaningful use of Gen AI that directly solves the problem. A functional, technically sound solution. Scalability and sustainability. The T&C wording is "Robustness, **Google Cloud architectural depth**, and technical execution" |
| **25%** | **Problem Alignment & Impact** | Fit to the chosen problem statement. Clear, meaningful value for users and the community. The T&C says "measurable real-world viability" |
| **25%** | **Innovation & Creativity** | Originality and differentiation. Gen AI used beyond a basic or superficial implementation |
| **10%** | **User Experience & Solution Design** | Intuitive, accessible to the intended users. The components work together cohesively |

- **An AI evaluation stage pre-screens submissions.** The deck and the description must state each rubric point explicitly.
- **No bonus points** are published for blogs, open source and so on.

## Mandatory tech
- The rules say: "Must leverage Google AI models (Gemini/Gemma) or agentic platforms (Agent Platform, Antigravity, AI Studio) **and** be deployed on Google Cloud via **Cloud Run or Firebase**."
- Solutions "built primarily on competing cloud infrastructure" are ineligible.
- **Submission page:** the brief description must explain how we use **Firebase, Firestore, Cloud Run and Gemini**. Treat all four as mandatory.
